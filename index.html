Case study · Health & wellness

A 90-second Meta ad
in under 48 hours.

A hiring-test brief from a health device brand: recreate a competitor's proven ad in the brand's own look, keep the script word for word, and deliver extra hooks to test. Built end to end with AI, from the first shot list to the final export.

01 · Breakdown

Why the reference works

Broke the competitor ad down shot by shot: hook, pacing, where each claim lands and when the product appears.

02 · Bible

One source of truth

Production bible with characters, locations, product details, brand colours and caption rules, so every AI shot stays consistent.

03 · Generate

31 shots, checked one by one

Realistic AI presenter for the A-roll, AI B-roll, X-ray overlays and 3D medical animation. Each clip checked for voice, continuity and product accuracy.

04 · Edit

Built for silent scrolling

Word-by-word karaoke captions, a numbered list that builds with each point, no music, and an end card in the brand's colours.

My own hooks, made to A/B test

The full 90-second ad was a private brand test, so it isn't posted here. Happy to walk you through it on a call.
